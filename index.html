About

Engineer by training, teacher by habit.

I studied Computer and Systems Engineering at Aswan University, graduating first in my class. My work sits where software meets hardware: microcontrollers, FPGA design, signal processing and machine learning.

Alongside my studies I taught programming, Arduino and digital fabrication to hundreds of students, and led APEL, Aswan's practical robotics student activity. Explaining a hard idea clearly has made me a better engineer.
